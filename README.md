## featured

- **[capstoneG12](https://github.com/mohamedNadyS/capstoneG12)** — spatio-temporal GNN (GAT + GRU) predicting traffic speeds on METR-LA: MAE 2.89 km/h, 7.79% MAPE. On top of it, an A*/Dijkstra routing engine with emergency-vehicle priority that routed 4,831 vehicles in ~2.2 seconds in its largest logged run, with emergency routes coming in ~50% cheaper than normal ones. SUMO-simulated, and served from a Raspberry Pi 5 to a Flutter app.
- **[textStudy](https://github.com/mohamedNadyS/textStudy)** — turns videos into summaries, notes (Markdown, LaTeX, PDF) and practice questions. Python, LLMs, Whisper, Transformers.
- **[ClipStack](https://github.com/mohamedNadyS/ClipStack)** — minimalist clipboard manager that tracks recent copies and exports them fast. Python, CustomTkinter.
- **[Video-summarizer](https://github.com/mohamedNadyS/Video-summarizer)** — transcribes video audio with Whisper and summarizes with BART, wrapped in a Streamlit UI.
